Privacy Policy

This page describes how FlowField Pro handles information. It is a product notice, not legal advice, and it has not been reviewed by an attorney.

Who we are

FlowField Pro is a field PWA for certified backflow testers. You use it for routes, tests, city PDF send, invoicing, and optional QuickBooks Online sync.

Contact: Cody.creek@msn.com

What we store

  • The email and password you use to sign in, plus the profile you save (name, company, phone, license, templates).
  • Customer, site, assembly, test, invoice, route, booking, and related field records you enter. On the demo and typical installs, that book lives on your device (browser storage / IndexedDB).
  • If you connect QuickBooks Online or use billed SaaS features, a FlowField Worker may hold OAuth tokens and billing entitlement so that connection can refresh. Those tokens are used only to talk to that service for your account.

Cookies and local storage

The PWA uses local storage, IndexedDB, and similar browser storage to keep you signed in, cache the app shell, and hold your field book. We do not use advertising cookies.

Third parties

  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online — only if you connect QBO. Intuit then processes the invoice data you choose to sync, under Intuit’s terms.
  • Stripe — if you subscribe to FlowField Pro SaaS billing.
  • Email providers (for example Resend or Postmark) — when you send a city or purveyor test report from the app.

We do not sell personal data.

Deletion

Email Cody.creek@msn.com and ask us to delete your account or any Worker-held token. On-device data can also be cleared from the browser or by wiping the demo book on that device.

Where we operate

FlowField Pro is offered from the United States, including Idaho.
